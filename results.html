How to use

Open any web page, let it load, then click the Page Saver icon in the toolbar. Page Saver finds every image at the biggest size the site has, and every video it can save, and shows them here so you can pick what to download.

Videos protected against copying (such as Netflix or Disney+) and live broadcasts can't be saved, and YouTube is not supported. Only save what you have the right to keep.
